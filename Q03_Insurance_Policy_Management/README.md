# Q03: Insurance Policy Management Solution

## Problem Statement
An insurance company wants to analyze customers, policies, premiums, claims, and policy types.

## Visuals Configured
1. **Headline Metrics**: Total Premium Collected, Total Claim Amount, Claim Ratio %.
2. **Clustered Bar Chart**: Premium by Policy Type.
3. **Clustered Column Chart**: Claims by Policy Type.
4. **Donut Chart**: Claims breakdown by Status (Approved, Pending, Rejected, No Claim).
5. **Interactive Policy Table**: Granular claim and premium listing.
