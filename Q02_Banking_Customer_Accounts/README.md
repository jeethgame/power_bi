# Q02: Banking Customer Account Analysis Solution

## Problem Statement
A bank maintains customer, branch, account, and transaction information and wants to analyze account balances and branch-level statistics.

## Visuals Configured
1. **KPI Cards**: Total Balance, Avg Balance, High Balance Accounts (> 100k).
2. **Clustered Bar Chart**: Total Balance by Branch.
3. **Donut Chart**: Balance distribution across Account Types (Savings, Current, FD, Salary).
4. **Column Chart**: Total Balance by Region.
5. **Interactive Table**: Granular customer and account list.
6. **Region Slicer**: Interactive filtering.
