# Q01: Hospital Patient Management Database Solution

## Problem Statement
A hospital maintains patient, doctor, department, appointment, and billing information and wants executive visual reports.

## What is in this Folder
- `Q01_Hospital_Patient_Management.pbip` - Double click to open directly in Power BI Desktop!
- `MockData.csv` - 120 sample hospital appointment & billing records.

## Visuals Configured
1. **Top KPI Cards**: Total Billing (`SUM`), Average Billing (`AVERAGE`), Total Patients (`COUNTROWS`).
2. **Clustered Bar Chart**: Department vs Total Billing.
3. **Clustered Column Chart**: Doctor vs Total Billing.
4. **Donut Chart**: Appointment Status distribution (Completed, Scheduled, Cancelled).
5. **Pie Chart**: Gender distribution.
6. **Department Slicer**: Interactively filter the entire report canvas.

## Examiner Talking Points
- Shows primary metrics aggregated by department and doctor.
- Slicer allows interactive cross-filtering across all visual types.
